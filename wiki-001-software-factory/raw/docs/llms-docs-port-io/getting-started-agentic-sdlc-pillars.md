# The pillars of an AI software factory

Port is the platform for building your organization's AI software factory - the system that runs your [agentic SDLC](/glossary/.md#agentic-sdlc) safely across every team. Six pillars make up the factory floor: a context lake that holds unified engineering knowledge, workflows that act on it, Port AI that guides the work, agent management that governs the agents doing it, an interface builder that keeps teams informed, and governance that enforces standards and access.

For the broader operating model, read the [AI software factory overview on port.io](https://www.port.io/glossary/ai-software-factory).

## Context lake[​](#context-lake "Direct link to Context lake")

The [context lake](/context-lake/overview/.md) is Port's unified engineering knowledge layer. It connects data from across your entire toolchain - repositories, cloud resources, incidents, deployments, and more - into a single, semantically-rich source of truth. Everything else in Port is powered by the context lake: AI agents reason over it, workflows act on it, and dashboards visualize it.

**In the onboarding:** You start building your context lake in [step 1](/getting-started/connect-your-git/.md) by connecting your Git provider, and continue in [step 4](/getting-started/set-up-your-context-lake/.md) by connecting more integrations and populating it with services and users.

## Workflows & tools[​](#workflows--tools "Direct link to Workflows & tools")

[Workflows](/workflows/overview/.md) provide a visual, node-based way to build automations and self-service experiences. You can chain multiple operations together, add conditional logic, and create complex automation flows triggered manually by users or automatically in response to catalog events.

**In the onboarding:** You build your first agentic workflow in [step 3](/getting-started/build-your-first-workflow/.md), and build more in [step 7](/getting-started/build-more-workflows/.md).

## Port AI[​](#port-ai "Direct link to Port AI")

The [Port AI assistant](/port-ai/overview/.md) is the conversational interface available throughout the product - it guides you through setup, helps you build workflows and dashboards, and answers questions about your catalog.

**In the onboarding:** The entire onboarding process is driven by the Port AI assistant, starting with [crafting your initial prompt](/getting-started/craft-an-initial-prompt/.md) in step 2.

## Agent management[​](#agent-management "Direct link to Agent management")

[Agent management](/agent-management/overview/.md) is the platform layer for building and governing AI agents across your SDLC - from custom agents that orchestrate engineering workflows, to an AI registry for managing skills, prompts, and MCP server access.

## Interface builder[​](#interface-builder "Direct link to Interface builder")

Port's [interface builder](/interface-builder/overview/.md) lets your team both surface and act on engineering data. Dashboards give a real-time view of what's happening across your environment - service health, deployment status, open incidents, and more - so each team sees exactly what matters to them. Teams can trigger workflows on demand, and approval flows ensure nothing important happens without the right people involved.

**In the onboarding:** You explore your use-case dashboards in [step 5](/getting-started/view-dashboards/.md) and can customize them at any time.

## Governance[​](#governance "Direct link to Governance")

[Governance](/governance/overview/.md) in Port covers two areas: access controls and standards enforcement. Access controls let you manage who can do what through RBAC, SSO, teams, and audit trails. Standards enforcement lets you define quality thresholds for your services using scorecards - covering security, reliability, production readiness, or any custom criteria - and track progress across your organization.

**In the onboarding:** You configure access control and define roles when you [invite your team](/getting-started/invite-users/.md) in step 6.
